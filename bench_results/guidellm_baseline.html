Recommendation: Run at ≈7.1 req/s — delivers 913 tok/s (67% of peak) while holding TTFT-p99 at 114ms, under the 200ms SLO. Pushing to peak adds only +50% throughput but raises p99 to 480ms and starts queueing. KV-cache peaks at just 5.4% — this run is compute-bound, not memory-bound, so there is headroom to raise --max-num-seqs / concurrency or batch size before memory is the limit.
